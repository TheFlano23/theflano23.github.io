Photo Uploader

Automatic desktop photo uploads to Google Photos.

Photo Uploader is a lightweight desktop application designed to automatically upload photos and videos from selected folders on a user's computer to their Google Photos library.

How it works

The application monitors folders selected by the user and uploads newly detected media directly to Google Photos using the official Google Photos API.

It uses append-only access and does not read, modify or delete existing items in the user's Google Photos library.

Authentication is handled through Google OAuth. Application data, authentication tokens and upload history are stored locally on the user's computer.
