Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Photo Uploader is a desktop application that allows users to automatically upload selected photos and videos to their own Google Photos account.

Google account access

Photo Uploader uses Google OAuth to authenticate the user and uses the Google Photos API to upload media.

The application requests append-only access to Google Photos. This permission allows the application to add new media to the user's Google Photos library.

Photo Uploader does not use this permission to read, modify or delete existing content in the user's Google Photos library.

Data collected

Photo Uploader does not operate a remote application server and does not collect or transmit personal information to the application's developer.

Media selected for upload is transmitted directly from the user's computer to Google's services through the Google Photos API.

Locally stored information

The application stores information locally on the user's computer in order to operate. This may include:

  • Google OAuth authentication tokens
  • Application configuration
  • Selected folder locations
  • Records of previously uploaded files
  • File hashes used to prevent duplicate uploads
  • Application logs

This information is not transmitted to the developer.

Sharing of information

Photo Uploader does not sell, rent or share user information with third parties.

Media and authentication information are communicated with Google only where necessary to provide the upload functionality requested by the user.

Data retention

Local application data remains on the user's computer until the user removes it or uninstalls and deletes the application's data.

Content uploaded to Google Photos is subject to the user's Google account settings and Google's own data retention policies.

Revoking access

Users may revoke Photo Uploader's access to their Google account at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change.
